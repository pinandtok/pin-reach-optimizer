About the Application

This application is developed specifically to leverage the official Pinterest developer framework to provide enhanced visual metrics and pin publishing pipelines. We strictly adhere to the Pinterest Developer Guidelines.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy clarifies how our developer application handles, processes, and protects your information when you authentic and connect your account via Pinterest OAuth.

Information We Collect

  • Pinterest Account Data: Upon explicit user authorization, we access user profile info (username, profile image) and basic account settings to personalize the UI dashboard.
  • Content and Metadata: We access specific Board titles, Pin descriptions, images, and performance analytics to compile user performance data.

How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly to fulfill your requested actions inside our platform (e.g., publishing a Pin to your board or pulling your board's metric analytics). We never sell, share, or redistribute your Pinterest access tokens or account insights to third-party entities.

Data Deletion & Revocation

You can revoke our app's access at any point directly via your Pinterest account's "Apps" security settings. Alternatively, you can contact our technical support directly through our repository portal to request formal deletion of cached metadata.
